Complaints resolution

If NICL has got it wrong, here is who decides.

A complaint goes first to the company, then to independent forums the law provides. None of them charges a policyholder to be heard.

1. The company

Write to the Complaints Officer at the office that issued the policy, or e-mail info@nicl.com.pk with "Complaint" in the subject line. Quote the policy or claim number. NICL acknowledges within three working days and replies in writing within fifteen.

2. Independent forums

Federal Insurance Ombudsman
Free, and independent of the insurer. Hears complaints of maladministration by an insurance company once the company has been given the chance to resolve the matter. Karachi.
SECP Insurance Division
The regulator's service desk takes complaints against any insurer registered under the Insurance Ordinance, 2000.
Small Disputes Resolution Committees
Constituted by the SECP for claims within the prescribed limit, for a quicker decision than a court.
Insurance Tribunal
For claims beyond the committees' limit, under Part XVI of the Insurance Ordinance.
Procurement complaints are differentA bidder's grievance goes to the Grievance Redressal Committee under the Public Procurement Rules. See the tender register.
