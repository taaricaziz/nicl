How to bid

  1. Download the bidding documents from the notice, or collect them from the procurement office named in it against the fee stated.
  2. Submit the technical and financial proposals in separately sealed envelopes, as the notice requires, before the closing time. Late bids are returned unopened.
  3. Attend the public opening at the time and place in the notice. Bids are read aloud and recorded.
  4. The evaluation report is published on PPRA and here at least ten days before the contract is awarded, as Rule 35 requires.

Grievance

A bidder aggrieved by any act of the procuring agency may lodge a written complaint with the Grievance Redressal Committee within ten days of the announcement of the evaluation report, under Rule 48 of the Public Procurement Rules, 2004.
